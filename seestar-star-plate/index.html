One arcsecond is 1/3600 of a degree (4.85 µrad): a 28 mm coin at about 5.8 km, a human hair at about 15 m, or about 29 cm at 60 km.

At the "Pixels" zoom, each square is one 4″ sensor pixel and a star's blur covers 2–3 of them. Barnard's Star's whole yearly parallax wobble fits inside a quarter of one pixel, so parallax work depends on fitting centroids to a few hundredths of a pixel and averaging hundreds of frames.
