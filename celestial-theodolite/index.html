Celestial theodolite · planar model

One star, two observers, a flat plane

At T = 0 the star sits on the peak's skyline from O₁. The observer then moves to the peak (O₂) at the same instant. On a plane the ground stays level and every vertical is parallel, so the only change to the star's elevation comes from where the observer stands.
